Sole Product Plan
Product & design proposal · v1 · 3 Oct 2026

Sole

A pedometer for iPhone that counts every step with the phone's own motion sensor, keeps your history safe in iCloud, and stays in step with Apple Health in both directions.

SwiftUI · iOS 17+CoreMotionHealthKitSwiftData + CloudKitNo third‑party code
Your four requirements

How Sole meets them

iPhone sensors

Counted by the motion chip

Steps come from CoreMotion's CMPedometer, the same low‑power motion coprocessor Apple uses. It counts all day even when Sole is closed.

Accuracy

No gaps, no double counts

Every launch backfills hour by hour from the sensor's 7‑day memory. When you also wear a Watch, each hour keeps the higher source only, so steps are never counted twice.

iCloud

Always there

History is stored with SwiftData synced to your private iCloud. A new iPhone or a reinstall brings every day back. No account to create.

Apple Health

Two‑way sync

Reads steps, distance and floors from Health (Watch, other apps, history). Writes Sole's hourly counts back. Only changes move each sync.

Screens

What it looks like

Three tabs: Today, History, Settings. Numbers are sample data. Mockups follow your light or dark setting, as the app will.

Under the hood

Where steps come from and go

One thing to know about writing to Health. iPhone already logs its own sensor steps into Health. Sole's samples sit alongside them and Health dedupes overlapping sources by priority, so your Health total will not double. You will simply see Sole listed as a step source too.

Plan

Build order

Each step is something you can run on your iPhone before the next starts.

  1. Data model and iCloud. SwiftData store with CloudKit, plus iCloud, HealthKit and App Group capabilities.
  2. Step engine. CMPedometer live updates and hourly backfill writing to the store.
  3. Today screen. The arc, stats and hourly bars on real data.
  4. Apple Health sync. Read and write with anchored queries and the per‑hour dedupe rule.
  5. History screen. Charts, streaks and day detail.
  6. Onboarding and Settings. Permissions flow, goal, units, sync status, export.
  7. Widgets. WidgetKit extension reading the shared store.

Before step 1: step counting does not work in the Simulator, so testing needs your iPhone. HealthKit and iCloud also need a paid Apple Developer account to enable those capabilities on com.pandyabhavik.Sole.
